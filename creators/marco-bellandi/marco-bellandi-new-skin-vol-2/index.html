Marco Bellandi / New Skin Vol.2

AI Music Video produced with Marco Bellandi out of the SYNTEZIS Milan studio floor.

Concept

"New Skin Vol.2" began as a single character beat and grew into a full ai music video — built around the editor as a recurring visual motif, positioned for a European audience first.

Visual System

A restrained palette pulled from the SYNTEZIS system — black, off-white and one controlled accent — applied across every frame, thumbnail and social cutdown to keep the identity consistent.

Production

Produced across 1 studio sessions using SYNTEZIS's AI image, video and 3D pipeline, with Marco Bellandi directing performance and tone in every review pass.
